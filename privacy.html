Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Nox Tarot is built so that your readings stay with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Nox Tarot has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

Your questions, readings, notes, daily cards and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Readings are put together on the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Motion

The app reads your device’s tilt so the cards can catch the light. This data is used on the spot and never stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders for your daily card and the end of a free trial are scheduled locally on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Nox Tarot does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
