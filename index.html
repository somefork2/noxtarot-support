Support

Nox Tarot is a tarot app for iPhone and iPad: a card of the day, seven spreads from one card to the Celtic Cross, full meanings for all 78 cards and a private reading journal. It comes with two decks, the classic 1909 art by Pamela Colman Smith and our own hand-drawn Nox deck.

Questions

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Nox Tarot, or in the app: Today › gear › Manage subscription. If you start a free trial, the app reminds you the day before it ends.

How do I restore a purchase? Tap “Restore” on the Nox Tarot Premium screen, or Today › gear › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I switch decks? Today › gear › Deck. Both decks share the same meanings.

Where is my journal stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Can tarot predict the future? Nox Tarot is for reflection and entertainment. The meanings follow long tarot tradition; they are not predictions or professional advice.
